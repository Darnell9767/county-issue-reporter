Help improve the roads we all pay for.

Darnell's App helps residents clearly document road and infrastructure problems so decision-makers can see where problems are actually happening.

⏱ Takes about 2 minutes

We will guide you through every step. You do not need to know road terminology.

Why report road problems?

Good information helps crews and administrators understand where maintenance is actually needed.

Repeated reports from the same area can help identify recurring problems instead of treating every report as an isolated event.

Step 1 of 6

Your location is one of the most important parts of the report.

Why are we asking?

A clear location lets crews find the exact problem instead of searching kilometres of road.

Step 3 of 6

📸 Show us the problem.

Good photos can explain a road problem better than a paragraph of text.

Why take pictures?

Photos help show the size, severity and surrounding conditions before someone is sent to inspect the area.

Take useful photos

1️⃣
Take one wide photo.

Show the whole road and surrounding area so people understand where the problem is.

2️⃣
Take one close photo.

Show the actual washboard, pothole, gravel buildup or damage.

☀️
Keep the sun behind you when possible.

Shooting directly into bright sunlight can hide the problem and make the photo nearly useless.

📏
Give the photo some scale.

Include part of the road, ditch, vehicle tire, sign or another safe reference when useful.

🦺
Safety first.

Do not stand in traffic or put yourself in danger just to get a photograph.

Step 4 of 6

🎙️ Tell us what you noticed.

Talk naturally. You do not need to write a formal report.

What should you mention?

Describe what you saw, how long the affected area is, whether it is getting worse, and anything that could affect safety or maintenance.

Example:

“The washboard starts about 200 metres west of the intersection and continues for roughly half a kilometre. The worst section is on the south side of the road.”
