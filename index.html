About this chat & your privacy

This is an AI companion. It can make mistakes and does not provide therapy, diagnoses or medication advice. The chat is not monitored by a person.

Your message and recent conversation are sent to OpenAI to generate a reply. The app server does not keep conversation records. The AI service's own data policies still apply.

Saving in this browser is optional. Turn it off to remove the app's saved copy, or use New chat to clear the current conversation. Other people using this browser may be able to read a saved chat.

A person can help, too.

A therapist, counsellor or healthcare provider can help you get ongoing support that fits your needs.
